🎓 About CollegeTools

Simple Tools for Student Life

CollegeTools is a free online platform designed to make everyday academic calculations easier and faster for students.

Our Purpose

Students frequently need to calculate CGPA, SGPA, percentages, attendance, required marks and other academic information. CollegeTools brings these useful tools together in one simple website.

Our goal is to provide calculators that are easy to understand, quick to use and accessible from both computers and mobile devices.

What You Can Find Here

🎓 Academic Calculators CGPA, SGPA and percentage calculations.
📅 Attendance Tools Check attendance and plan classes.
📚 Study Planning Tools Calculate required marks and predict CGPA.
💼 Placement Tools Compare your details with entered placement requirements.

Our Approach

We aim to keep CollegeTools straightforward. You should be able to open a tool, enter your information and understand the result without unnecessary complexity.

The website will continue to improve with useful student-focused information, explanations and tools.

Important Information

CollegeTools provides calculations and general educational information for convenience. Results should be checked against your college, university or company's official rules when those rules apply.
