About CollegeTools

Simple, free and useful online tools designed to help college students with everyday academic calculations and planning.

Our Purpose

CollegeTools provides free online calculators and educational resources for college students. Our aim is to make common academic calculations easier to understand and perform.

Students can use our tools to calculate grades, check attendance, estimate required marks and explore other academic calculations without needing to install additional software.

Our Tools

CollegeTools includes a range of student-focused tools, such as:

  • CGPA Calculator
  • SGPA Calculator
  • Percentage Calculator
  • Attendance Calculator
  • Bunk Calculator
  • Age Calculator
  • AI Face Age Estimator
  • Marks Required Calculator
  • CGPA Predictor
  • Placement Eligibility Checker

Each tool is intended to make a specific task more convenient. Results depend on the accuracy of the information entered by the user.

Our Approach

We aim to make CollegeTools easy to navigate, accessible across devices and straightforward to use.

We also aim to improve the website over time by addressing issues and making our tools more useful for students.

Accuracy and Responsible Use

We aim to provide useful and accurate calculations. However, different colleges and universities may follow different grading systems, attendance policies and academic rules.

Please verify important academic results with your institution or another official source. Predictions and estimates should not be treated as guaranteed outcomes.
