Learn More About Your Results

Simple explanations, formulas and examples to help you understand your academic calculations.

🎓

What is CGPA?

CGPA stands for Cumulative Grade Point Average. It represents your overall academic performance across multiple semesters or subjects.

CGPA Formula

CGPA = Σ (Grade Point × Credits) ÷ Σ Credits

For example, if a subject has a grade point of 8 and 4 credits, its contribution is 8 × 4 = 32 grade points.

📘

What is SGPA?

SGPA stands for Semester Grade Point Average. It measures your academic performance for a particular semester.

SGPA Formula

SGPA = Σ (Grade Point × Credits) ÷ Σ Credits

Higher credit subjects have a greater effect on your SGPA because their grade points are weighted by credits.

📊

How is Percentage Calculated?

Percentage tells you how many marks you obtained compared with the total marks available.

Percentage Formula

Percentage = (Obtained Marks ÷ Total Marks) × 100

Example: If you score 420 out of 500, your percentage is 84%.

📅

How Attendance is Calculated

Your attendance percentage is based on the number of classes you attended compared with the total classes held.

Attendance Formula

Attendance = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes) × 100

You can use our calculator to find out how many additional classes you need to attend to reach your target attendance.

How Does CGPA Prediction Work?

The CGPA Predictor estimates your future CGPA using your current CGPA, completed credits, expected SGPA and upcoming semester credits.

Weighted Formula

Predicted CGPA = (Current Points + Next Semester Points) ÷ Total Credits

This is an estimate and assumes the entered SGPA and credit values are accurate.
