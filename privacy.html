Your Privacy Matters

This Privacy Policy explains how CollegeTools handles information when you visit and use our website.

1. Introduction

CollegeTools provides free educational and academic calculation tools for students. We aim to explain transparently how information may be collected and used through our website.

2. Information We Collect

Most calculators are designed to perform calculations in your browser. Information entered into a calculator is used to provide the requested result.

Users do not need to create an account simply to use the calculators.

If you contact us by email, we may receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message.

Our hosting provider and technical services may process technical information such as browser requests, IP addresses and server logs as part of operating and securing the website.

3. Calculator Information

Common academic calculations are designed to run directly in your browser. Do not enter unnecessary sensitive or confidential information into website tools.

If a feature uses an external service, the relevant information may be processed according to that service's policies. Please review the details of a feature before using it.

4. Cookies and Advertising

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to support website functionality, understand website usage or provide advertising, depending on the services enabled on this website.

If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalize ads as permitted by applicable settings and policies.

Users can learn more about Google's advertising practices and available controls in Google's advertising and privacy documentation.

More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps .

5. Third-Party Services

CollegeTools may use third-party providers for hosting, advertising, analytics, content delivery and other website functions.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies. Please consult those policies for further details about their practices.

6. AI Face Age Estimator

CollegeTools includes an AI-powered face age estimation feature. The feature is designed to process images using client-side code where supported by the implementation.

We do not claim that every image-processing operation is necessarily performed locally. The handling of an image may depend on the feature's implementation and any external services used.

The output is an estimate of apparent age, not identity verification or a reliable determination of actual age. Only use images you have permission to use.

7. Children's Privacy

CollegeTools is intended for general educational use. We do not intentionally request personal information from children through the calculators.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to support the security of the website. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Choices

You can choose not to provide information through optional communications and can stop using a website feature at any time.

Cookie and advertising controls may also be available through your browser, Google's settings or a consent tool where applicable.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when CollegeTools, its features or applicable requirements change. Updated information will be published on this page.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact page .
