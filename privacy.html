🔒 Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters

This Privacy Policy explains how CollegeTools handles information when you visit and use our website.

1. Introduction

CollegeTools provides free educational and academic calculation tools for students. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information may be collected and used.

2. Information We Collect

Most of the calculators on CollegeTools work directly in your browser. Information entered into a calculator is generally used to perform the requested calculation.

We do not ask users to create an account simply to use the calculators.

If you contact us by email, we may receive the information that you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and the contents of your enquiry.

3. Calculator Information

CollegeTools is designed so that common academic calculations can be performed directly in the browser.

Users should avoid entering unnecessary sensitive or confidential information into website tools.

4. Cookies

CollegeTools may use cookies or similar technologies in the future to improve website functionality, understand website usage or support advertising services.

If third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense are used, those services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

5. Third-Party Services

CollegeTools may use third-party services for features such as analytics, advertising, hosting, content delivery or other website functionality.

These third-party services may collect information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. AI Face Age Estimator

CollegeTools includes an AI-powered face age estimation feature. Images selected for this feature are processed by the website's client-side functionality where supported.

The feature provides an estimated apparent age and should not be considered an identification, verification or exact age determination service.

Users should avoid uploading photographs that they do not have permission to use.

7. Children's Privacy

CollegeTools is intended primarily for students and general educational use. We do not intentionally request personal information from children.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the website. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to CollegeTools, its features or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact page .
