Your Privacy Matters

This Privacy Policy explains how CollegeTools handles information when you visit and use our website.

1. Introduction

CollegeTools provides free educational and academic calculation tools for students. This policy describes the types of information that may be processed when you use our website, including information handled by third-party services.

2. Information We Collect

Most academic calculators are designed to perform calculations in your browser. The values you enter are used to calculate and display results.

You do not need to create an account to use the calculators.

If you contact us by email or through our contact page, we may receive your email address, message, and any other information you choose to provide.

Our hosting provider and third-party technical services may process technical information, such as IP addresses, browser details, request information, and server logs, to deliver, maintain, and secure the website.

3. Calculator Information

Standard academic calculations are designed to run directly in your browser. Do not enter unnecessary sensitive, confidential, or personally identifying information into website tools.

The way information is processed may differ between features. Features that use external libraries, content delivery networks, or other third-party services may cause technical information to be sent to those providers when their resources are requested.

4. Cookies and Advertising

CollegeTools may use advertising services such as Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies to serve advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, and personalize ads where permitted by applicable settings and requirements.

The information processed by Google and its partners is subject to their own policies and applicable privacy settings. The use of advertising technologies may depend on your location, browser settings, consent, and the services enabled on the website.

You can learn more about Google's advertising practices and available controls here:

How Google uses information from sites or apps

Where required, appropriate consent mechanisms and advertising settings should be used for visitors in applicable regions.

5. Third-Party Services

CollegeTools may rely on third-party providers for website hosting, content delivery, advertising, and other technical functions. These providers may process information when your browser requests their resources.

Our AI Face Age Estimator, for example, uses a third-party JavaScript library and machine-learning model files delivered through public content delivery networks.

Third-party providers operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage users to review those policies to understand how the providers handle technical information.

6. AI Face Age Estimator

CollegeTools includes an AI-powered Face Age Estimator that estimates a person's apparent age from a user-selected image.

Based on the current website implementation, face detection and age estimation are performed in the user's browser using JavaScript and machine-learning models. The selected image is displayed and processed by the browser. The current implementation does not appear to send the selected image to a CollegeTools server or a separate AI-processing API.

The feature loads its JavaScript library and model files from third-party content delivery networks. Those providers may process technical information associated with requests for their resources, subject to their own privacy policies.

The estimated age is approximate and may be inaccurate. It is not identity verification and should not be treated as a reliable determination of a person's actual age.

Only use images you have permission to use. Avoid uploading sensitive or confidential images. You can choose not to use this feature.

7. Children's Privacy

CollegeTools is intended for general educational use. The calculators do not require an account or intentionally request personal information to provide calculation results.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through our contact channels, please contact us so that we can review the request.

8. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable measures to support the security and proper operation of the website. However, no website, internet transmission, or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Choices

You can choose not to submit information through optional communications and can stop using any website feature at any time.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Additional advertising preferences or consent controls may be available through Google or a consent tool where applicable. Disabling certain technologies may affect some website or advertising functionality.

10. External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We do not control the privacy practices or content of those websites. Review their policies before providing personal information.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our website, features, third-party services, or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised update date.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact page .
